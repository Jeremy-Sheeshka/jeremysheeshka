<div class="dropdown-menu">
<div class="dropdown-group">

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Introduction</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">One of our projects for ETEC 511: New Foundations of Educational Technology was to identify a significant shift and technological change in an educational technology where the adoption of a newer technology displaces a previous one. <br><br>
    For this group project, we have chosen to focus on the small-scale tipping point case of the technology adoption of Mathology through the lenses of: differentiated learning, differentiated instruction, and differentiated assessment.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">What is Mathology?</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">Mathology is a digital and print math resource that shifts learning from traditional paper-and-pencil tasks to more hands-on, engaging, and accessible learning experiences. This digital platform blends core instructional strategies with tools that allow for differentiated instruction and assessment in real-time. Inspired by the idea of “Mathland,” Mathology helps create inclusive, accessible, student-centered classrooms where every learner can thrive.</p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/mathologylogo.png" alt="Mathology Logo" style="display:block; margin: 10px auto; max-width: 200px;" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">What systems or tools does it displace?</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">Mathology represents a cross-curricular shift in the elementary math classroom by displacing traditional teaching tools—like dice, playing cards, cubes, and textbooks—with digital and printed counterparts. It transcends analog resource management and displaces paper-and-pencil assessments through a single multi-modal platform.</p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/tpticeberg.png" alt="Tipping Point Iceberg" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Usability of Mathology</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;"><strong>Working with Woolgar</strong><br><br>
    The instantaneous feedback, along with the prescribed sequences and actions of how students are able to interact with the on-screen material (dragging, clicking, uncovering etc.), inside of a story-driven environment, are all design choices that effectively make Mathology rise above the usability of traditional tools and systems.
    </p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/mathologyintroduction.png" alt="Mathology Introduction" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">COVID-19: A Catalyst for Change</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">The COVID-19 pandemic marked a critical tipping point for digital educational resources. School systems across the globe shifted from in-person to online learning. UNESCO (2020) reported that the pandemic triggered a “world crisis in education,” which led to global efforts including digital innovation to maintain student engagement.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Sustainability & Digital Labour</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">From a sustainability perspective, Mathology's long-term potential can be examined across three key dimensions:</p>
    <div class="sustainability-cards">
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card-title">Student</div>
        <div class="card-body">
          <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">Creates sustainable pathways by meeting students at their level, preventing discouragement while offering multiple instruction methods.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card-title">Teacher</div>
        <div class="card-body">
          <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">Sustains educator capacity by streamlining routine tasks like grading with actionable data insights, reducing workload burdens.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card-title">Systemic</div>
        <div class="card-body">
          <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">Offers a way to improve math education quality across grades while reducing paper waste, despite challenges with for-profit monopolization.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
