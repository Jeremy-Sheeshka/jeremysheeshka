<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">What is Mathology?</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">Mathology is a digital and print math resource that shifts learning from traditional paper tasks to hands-on, engaging experiences. It supports differentiated learning by meeting students where they are at.</p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/mathologylogo.png" alt="Mathology Logo" style="display:block; margin: 10px auto; max-width: 200px;" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">What systems or tools does it displace?</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p style="color: var(--theme-text) !important;">Mathology represents a shift in physical artifacts—dice, cards, blocks, and textbooks—focusing instead on an accessible, multi-modal digital platform.</p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/tpticeberg.png" alt="Tipping Point Iceberg" />
