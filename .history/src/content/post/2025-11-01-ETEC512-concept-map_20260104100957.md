<strong>Tool Selection and Rationale</strong><br><br>
For my concept map, I chose to use the D3.js JavaScript library (version 7, free) to create an interactive, force-directed graph. My decision to do this was driven by a desire to try something new and go beyond a static mind-map by creating a dynamic representation reflective of the complex and constantly shifting relationship between theories that have gone on to shape my personal perspective towards learning.
<br><br>

<strong>How I Used the Tool and AI</strong><br><br>
This project ultimately came down to having an idea, researching the logistics of how to implement it, and then using a combination of Generative AI tools (including ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Lovable.Dev, and Gemini - free versions) to help me construct and implement a workable D3.js template.
<br><br>

<strong>Challenges and Problem-Solving</strong><br><br>
For this assignment, the Generative AI tools were primarily used for mass editing and to manage the consistency of the visualization of data.
<br><br>

<strong>Keeping My Mind Front and Center</strong><br><br>
In keeping my mind front and center throughout this process, I feel I maintained agency over my use of AI through pre-determining the main and sub-branches of each of the nodes.
<br><br>

<strong>Reflection and Learning</strong><br><br>
While it's not perfect by any means, I am happy with my choice to try out something new and create something indicative of the complex and ever-shifting perspective I hold towards how learning takes place.
