## Tipping Point

**A Project by Noralee Yarra, Jordan Swallow, and Jeremy Sheeshka**

<div class="dropdown-menu">
<div class="dropdown-group">

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Introduction</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p> One of our projects for ETEC 511 was to identify a significant shift where a newer educational technology displaces a previous one. We focused on Mathology through the lenses of differentiated learning, instruction, and assessment.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">What is Mathology?</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p>Mathology is a digital and print resource that shifts learning from traditional paper tasks to hands-on, accessible experiences. It supports differentiated learning by meeting students at their current proficiency levels. Inspired by Papert's "Mathland," it creates inclusive, student-centered classrooms.</p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/mathologylogo.png" alt="Mathology Logo" style="max-width:200px; display:block; margin: 0 auto;"/>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">What systems or tools does it displace?</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p>Mathology displaces traditional tools like calculators, dice, and physical worksheets by bundling workflow, delivery, and assessment into a single multi-modal platform.</p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/tpticeberg.png" alt="Tipping Point Iceberg" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Usability of Mathology</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p><strong>Working with Woolgar:</strong> Mathology considers user experience by offering clickable manipulatives and real-time dashboards, effectively rising above the usability of traditional analog systems.</p>
    <img src="/assets/images/blog/mathologyintroduction.png" alt="Mathology Introduction" />
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">The Tipping Point & COVID-19</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p>The pandemic was a critical catalyst, pushing educators to embrace digital platforms for mathematical modelling. Mathology’s prototype launched in 2019 and became central to remote learning in 2020.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Differentiated Education</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p>Mathology uses algorithms to deliver a personalized experience. As Seymour Papert posited, math should be learned naturally, like language, rather than through memorization.</p>
    <div style="text-align: center; margin: 1rem 0;">
      <iframe width="100%" style="aspect-ratio: 16/9;" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/6-dFTmdX1kU?start=360" title="YouTube video" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Digital Labour & Sustainability</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p>From a sustainability perspective, Mathology's potential can be examined across three key dimensions:</p>
    
    <div class="sustainability-cards">
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card-title">Student</div>
        <div class="card-body">
          <p>Creates sustainable pathways by preventing discouragement and offering multiple instruction methods like digital interactives and hands-on activities.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card-title">Teacher</div>
        <div class="card-body">
          <p>Sustains capacity by streamlining grading and lesson planning, reducing the unsustainable workload burden on educators.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <div class="card-title">Systemic</div>
        <div class="card-body">
          <p>Improves education quality across grades and reduces paper waste, though it faces challenges regarding monopolistic for-profit structures.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="dropdown-item">
  <div class="dropdown-question">Conclusion</div>
  <div class="dropdown-answer">
    <p>Mathology's emergence during the pandemic signals a critical tipping point. It is not just about the arrival of something new, but the value it carries for users and the equitable possibilities it opens for the future.</p>
  </div>
</div>
