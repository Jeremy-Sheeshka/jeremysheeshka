## What media ecology is

Media ecology, as Strate (2000) frames it, is the study of media environments, and Lum (2000) extends this to the study of a collection of mediums in an environment as a whole. It is the intersection between various technologies coming together alongside semiotic meaning, where a medium's physical and symbolic form carry built-in biases, among them intellectual, emotional, spatial, political and social, that come to form the realities people come to know and operate within (Lum, 2000).

The element of ecology within the study of media ecology highlights the reciprocal relationship between how media act on one another as well as on us to become a combination of how technics and living things come to share and influence a singular habitat as a whole. Media ecology has us consider how various mediums together influence and interact with one another alongside the overarching accumulative environmental conditions and the effect they have on inhabitants as they operate from day to day. This is what allows media ecology to ask the right questions: not whether a medium works, but what kind of world it builds and what it makes visible or hidden to those inside it.

## A classroom as a media environment

An educational media ecology perspective treats the classroom as "structured spaces of special-purpose inhabitation" de Castell, et al (p.77) that is described as the: buildings, light, air, furniture, bells, paper, roles and institution, coming together whereby a small change can ripple many elements within the environment.

Who decided where you sit? What has this room decided you will be able to see? Whose time does it keep? What is the building doing to the bodies in it? These are the observations I have come to recognize in my own space as it currently is pictured above. While I have only identified a handful of objects in my depiction of the classroom as a media environment, the arcs between each medium such as the bell and the birthday chart, become the ecology we interact with. Whether media ecology is a science, a philosophy or a social criticism is one of Postman's open questions that sticks with me and has me yearning to untangle the mediums and messages I share in this space.
