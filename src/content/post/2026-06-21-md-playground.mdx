## 1. Basic Text Formatting

- **Bold** and *italic* and ***bold-italic***.
- ~~Strikethrough~~ (use `~~`).
- Inline `code` with backticks.
- `==Highlighted text==` (Obsidian) or `<mark>HTML</mark>`.
- Subscript: H~2~O (`~`) or H<sub>2</sub>O (`<sub>`)
- Superscript: X^2^ (`^`) or X<sup>2</sup> (`<sup>`)
