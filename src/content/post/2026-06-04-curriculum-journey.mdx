    <p>
<i>Currere</i> is William Pinar's method for examining educational experience through four orientations: the regressive (returning to the past), the progressive (imagining the future), the analytical (holding both in tension), and the synthetical (returning to the present, changed).
Below is a digital visualization of my curriculum journey structured through those four orientations creating a transmodal blend of cyberspace and physical place. By intertwining a natural landscape with acoustic guitar and CSS animations, this assignment reveals my personal conception of curriculum through aural, visual, and spatial means.

Each layer of the image holds one orientation of <i>currere</i>. Click the hotspots to read, listen, and experience.
</p>
