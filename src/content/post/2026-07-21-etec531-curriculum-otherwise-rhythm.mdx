# Curriculum Otherwise

Sight Reading Factory is a tool that lets users practice their rhythms through generated exercises of varying difficulty. Our group has designed a reimagining of that platform, with the hope of being more accessible by giving effective feedback and, through our added features, challenging the normative way of reading music.

The goal of this Doing is to engage with both tools and write a reflection on our experience of using each tool and comparing them to each other, as well as providing feedback on what is missing or what can be improved. Rhythm Reader was what we collectively came up with.
