My artifact is Dinosaur Mode, a little rhythm game I built as a secret tribute to chrome://dino — you tap on the metronome beat to clear scrolling notation, and the click is the only thing that tells you when you're right. Overtly it teaches rhythm and sight-reading, but what it really taught me is that learning sticks when the stakes are low enough to play with: I built it to be something you stumble into and feel invited by, not tested by, and that openness is the curriculum I want to carry into my own teaching.
