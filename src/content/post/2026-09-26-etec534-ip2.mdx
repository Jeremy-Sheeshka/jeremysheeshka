<b>Workflow and Process:</b>
I began by researching alternatives to PowerPoint presentations. I had previously used the HTML presentation framework reveal.js for another course, so I decided to explore it further and found a framework based on reveal.js called Quarto. [I proceeded by finding a starter template that I liked](https://emitanaka.org/slides/toronto2022/#/title-slide) on the [quarto.org](https://quarto.org) website, and had AI (Deepseek v4 Flash via Deepseek Harness) assist with cloning the template and wiring up a starting point within my Astro blog that fit the five-slide requirement for IP#2.

Next, I researched whiteout products, their applications in different industries, and their relationships to the landscape of technologies they coincided with throughout history. I pursued different images, advertisements, and comics to use in my slides which utilized whiteout, specifically seeking out examples where it was visibly applied as an imperfect corrective tool.

I then began drafting the written content for each of the slides, aiming to untangle corrective-fluid/film technologies as a whole in relation to other evolving tools that have paralleled and/or come to displace, develop, and influence our connections to other educational technologies (and each other) over time.

With the help of AI, I amalgamated the content I created into the five-slide format and template I had previously drafted, and combed through it several times to create the animated transitions between each of the slides that coincided with my conception of how I wanted IP#2 to take place.

[I had previously found and saved a hand-drawn React component library on GitHub](https://github.com/SarthakRawat-1/sketchbook-ui) that I wanted to style my slides and content with, and then had AI (Claude Opus 5.5 via Claude Code) implement the UI library in a way that resembled the thematic use of whiteout in the pseudo-analog sketchbook style I envisioned.

After establishing the layout, written draft, and aesthetic I was after, I began fine-tuning the overall appearance of my draft, using AI to manipulate the Quarto framework I had chosen in order to create the desired flow within each slide.

Throughout this process, my intention in using AI for this Intellectual Production was to supplement and extend my knowledge and capabilities using technologies I was both familiar and unfamiliar with, in order to experiment and try something new. All final wording and choices regarding the content presented within this slideshow were conceptualized by me, and are shared as a depiction of my own understanding of the course content. Overall, the majority of my AI usage focused on the visual organization of my materials and the aesthetic alignment of the slideshow so that it would be representative of the medium itself (lined paper, sticky notes, and "cut-outs" of the images chosen).
</Disclosure>
