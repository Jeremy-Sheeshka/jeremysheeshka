# Introducing Curator

---

## The Problem

Institutions sit on knowledge they can't safely hand to cloud AI: lesson recordings, student records, parent notes, billing information. In BC, privacy considerations make it a real liability to hand student or client data to a public cloud AI. No major AI product was built with that liability in mind, or with a way to integrate into the offline tech stacks these institutions already run.

<div class="clear-both"></div>


## Curator: The Local AI Infrastructure 

Curator is open-source, local-first AI infrastructure that runs on hardware a school or studio already owns, so that knowledge never has to leave the building to be useful.

<div class="md:float-right md:w-1/4 md:ml-6 md:mb-4 w-3/4 sm:w-1/2 mx-auto md:mx-0 mb-6 mt-2">
  <Figure
    src="/assets/images/blog/curatorengine.png"

  <p class="text-xs text-center text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400 mt-2">Curator Engine runs entirely on local hardware</p>
</div>

Rather than sending data to the cloud for processing, Curator brings the AI to the data. It is designed to be deployed on the existing hardware a school or studio already has, keeping sensitive information like student records, lesson content, and billing data within the institution's physical control.

This is not a cloud product with a privacy policy that promises not to look at your data. It is infrastructure that makes it structurally impossible for your data to leave the building unless you explicitly choose to share it.

<div class="clear-both"></div>

---

## Cadenza: The Flagship Application

Cadenza, our first application of this technology, shows what that looks like in practice: it listens to a lesson locally, drafts a summary, retrieves and transposes the right sheet music, answers voice questions against your own uploaded documents, and shares curated information with clients securely.

<div class="md:float-left md:w-1/4 md:mr-6 md:mb-4 w-3/4 sm:w-1/2 mx-auto md:mx-0 mb-6">
  <Figure
    src="/assets/images/blog/cascaderesources.png"
    alt="Cadenza Studio resources interface"
  />
</div>

For example, you might ask it to find the Clarinet 3 part in a concert band arrangement and email it to two of your students. It's that easy. No digging through a filing cabinet. No drafting any emails. No trip to the photocopier!

Cadenza is our flagship application and technological wedge, which we are bringing to market, of Curator. It demonstrates that local-first AI is not a compromise or a stepping stone toward something cloud-based. It is the destination: a model where institutions own their data, own their infrastructure, and still get the full power of AI.

<div class="clear-both"></div>
