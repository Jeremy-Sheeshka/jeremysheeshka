**Math Champion** is a competitive "Around the World" style math game built for classroom smartboards. Two or three students go head-to-head answering addition, subtraction, multiplication, or odd/even questions. First player to tap the correct answer wins the round and stays at the board while the other player(s) switch. Complete a streak of correct answers to become the champion!
