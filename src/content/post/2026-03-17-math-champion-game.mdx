  <div style="flex: 1;">
    <p>Math Champion is a competitive math game that was imagined and built for classroom smartboards for lower elementary students.</p>
    <p>It is meant to be played as a "round the world" style with two or three students at a time going head-to-head taking turns doing math questions.</p>
    <p>The first player to tap the correct answer wins the round and stays at the board while the other player(s) switch. The goal is to complete a streak of correct answers in a row in order to be deemed the math champion.</p>
