Math Champion is a competitive math game that was imagined for built for classroom smartboards for lower elementary students.
It is meant to be played as a "round the world" style with two or three students at a time going head-to-head taking turns doing math questions. 
The First player to tap the correct answer wins the round and stays at the board while the other player(s) switch. The goal is to complete a streak of correct answers in a row in order to be deemed the math champion.
