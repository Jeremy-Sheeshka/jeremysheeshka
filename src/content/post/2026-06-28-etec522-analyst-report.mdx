# Sight Reading Factory: An Opportunity Audit

<Prose>
For my Educational Venture Analysis, I looked at Sight Reading Factory, a tool that generates unlimited sight-reading exercises for music students based on various parameters. I chose it because sight-reading is an important part of music literacy and is an ongoing music skill that transfers over to ensemble work, auditions, instrumental proficiency, and other core musical competencies.

In the role of a music educator, I myself have never used this tool before in the classroom. This task was an opportunity for me to research into and report on Sight Reading Factory in the capacity of an Educational Venture Analyst in order to examine the value it offers and whether it is an investment candidate for a potential investor in the realm of contemporary music education technologies.

My analysis includes identifying market opportunities and risks, reviews, and a structured EVA score relevant to the venture’s team, concept, marketability, and plan.

Do useful classroom technologies always allign as sound long-term investments?
