# Sight Reading Factory: An Opportunity Audit

<Prose>
For my Educational Venture Analysis, I looked at Sight Reading Factory, a tool that generates unlimited sight-reading exercises for music students based on various parameters. I chose it because sight-reading is an important part of music literacy, a skill that transfers to ensemble work, auditions, instrumental proficiency, and other core musical competencies.

As a music educator, I had never used this tool in the classroom. This was an opportunity for me to research and report on Sight Reading Factory in the capacity of an Educational Venture Analyst, to examine whether it is a sound investment for investors in music education technology.

My analysis includes identifying market opportunities and risks, reviews, and a structured EVA score relevant to the venture's team, concept, marketability, and plan. 

Do useful educational technologies always align as sound long-term investments?
