<i>Slow AI</i> is the 2025 virtual exhibition from Leonardo/ISAST's CripTech AI Lab — seven projects by disabled artists, hackers, musicians, and teachers from seven countries, all working against an industry built for speed. Instead of scraped data and distant data centers, the group reached for cellular automata, locally-run open-source models, and their own writing, paintings, and medical records.
Below is my recreation of the original exhibition's structure and design: a winding path of seven stones, each one a project, moving at what curator M Eilo calls crip time.

## A note on sources

This recreation rebuilds the layout, structure, and accessibility features of the original — including its animation toggle, which felt important to preserve given the exhibition's disability-justice framing. The seven project visuals in my version are original abstract patterns, not the exhibition's own photography, and the project descriptions are written in my own words rather than copied from the curatorial text.
