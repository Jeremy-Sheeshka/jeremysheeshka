# Integrated Adaptive Learning & Assistive Technology

For ETEC 522's Opportunity Forecast assignment, my team built an open educational resource on adaptive learning technology and its intersection with IEPs. Research, design, facilitate, revise: the full cycle.

The OER is published under a Creative Commons license. If you teach, design instruction, or work anywhere near the IEP process, take a look.
