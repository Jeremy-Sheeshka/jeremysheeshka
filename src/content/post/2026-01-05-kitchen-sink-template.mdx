## 9) Steps component (procedures / methods)

<Steps title="A repeatable writing workflow">
  <Step><b>Name</b> the old practice you're describing.</Step>
  <Step><b>Describe</b> the new tool and what it bundles.</Step>
  <Step><b>Identify</b> what gets displaced (materials, routines, assessments).</Step>
  <Step><b>Analyze</b> the labour shift (teacher + student work).</Step>
  <Step><b>Flag</b> the equity hinge: who benefits, who gets excluded.</Step>
  <Step><b>Conclude</b> with tradeoffs + references.</Step>
</Steps>
